This map shows how PsychArmor likely produces its video courses and weekly podcast, built from the PsychArmor website and our 22 September correspondence, and not yet checked with staff. Confirmed from the site: the 12 to 18 month course timeline, stakeholder surveys and interviews, SME advisory panel review, in-house design team, staff roles, Duane France as podcast host and producer, Tuesday release cadence, podcast episodes used as resource material for the course library, and machine-style transcripts posted with raw speaker labels and errors. Everything marked with an asterisk, including tools, equipment, owners, delays, and dashed connections, is our assumption. Gold steps are where human judgment protects quality and safety on suicide-prevention and clinical content. Red steps are hypothesized time costs. The longest waits sit in research and review, the podcast runs largely on one person, and the two processes share people, promotion, and a transcription burden.
