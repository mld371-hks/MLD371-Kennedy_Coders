This map shows how PsychArmor likely hires for a full-time role, using its posted Learning Engagement & Partnership Manager opening as the example. Almost every step is our assumption: PsychArmor's careers page lists open roles and a "View Job Description" link, but does not describe how applications are received, screened, or decided. Roles come from the team page; the reporting line for the posted Learning Engagement & Partnership Manager role is confirmed. Everything marked with an asterisk, including tools, owners, steps, and delays, must be confirmed with staff. Gold steps are where human judgment matters most, such as interviews for mission fit. Red steps are hypothesized time costs. Steps outlined in blue are where the proposed screening tool would plug in, with a person still making every decision.

Sources: psycharmor.org/careers and /team, accessed 8 Oct 2026. California Civil Rights Council automated-decision system regulations, effective 1 Oct 2025 (summaries: Mayer Brown, CalChamber, Akin Gump). Color scheme follows the Studio 5 prompt.
